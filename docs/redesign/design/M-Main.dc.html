Why four tabs: each one is a different moment — driving, looking back, timing, setup — so they stay separate. Two renames: Dash → Live so it isn’t confused with the dash itself, and Track → Laps because it says what you get. Before connecting, the app is one Scan screen (with Demo mode and App info); no tabs until a dash is linked.

What wins — overlays, top first

  1. Critical alert — full screen, solid red. Every active alert is listed; acknowledging one never hides another.
  2. Reconnect failed — dialog: Try again · Scan for dashes.
  3. Reconnecting banner — under the header, with Cancel (back to Scan). Values turn stale.
  4. Acknowledged / muted alerts — red strip under the header while the problem lasts.
  5. Phone battery low — orange banner, dismissable.
Landscape on the mount: the tab bar becomes a left rail; overlays behave the same.

The state is always written in words (Warning, Danger, “8 s ago”), not shown by colour alone. Stale values turn grey with a dashed edge and their age — they never keep looking live.

Glance rules

Numbers
Monospace, 44 px+ on Live, 72 px+ for the lap chrono.
Targets
44 px minimum; 56 px on driving screens; 88 px for Capture lap and Acknowledge (gloves).
Words
Same as the Tuner: Connected / Reconnecting… / Not connected. Say what happened, then what to do.
Contrast
Text on dark ≥ 4.5:1; muted grey only for secondary info.
